### Tiny Experiments

I've had this book in my library for almost two years, but I never had the time to read it, although I try to average two books a month, I still didn't find time for it.

The book focuses on the idea of using tiny experiments instead of rigid goals. This method focuses on the output, rather than the outcome. You don't set a goal of reaching 10K followers for your newly created channel, but you say I would publish two videos a week for the next 100 days, after this time is over you evaluate your results and based on that you decide if you want to continue or not. The output is ~30 videos published, the outcome is how successful this was.

Of course the book contains many other interesting ideas, like chapter 5 when the author talks more about procrastination, if you're not a native English speaker like me then I will let you know that "procrastination is the act of unnecessarily delaying, postponing, or putting off a task or decision despite knowing that doing so will likely lead to negative consequences". Anne mentions that the source of procrastination could be either head, heart, or hand. She suggests to ask yourself these three questions to understand more why you are procrastinating. Is the task appropriate? exciting? and doable?

I am looking forward to what this book still has to offer, I will also try to write a short summary of it.
