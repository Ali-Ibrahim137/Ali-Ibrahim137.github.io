## Summary

This week I focused mostly on preparing the required documents for my master's applications. I also finished reading "Men Are from Mars, Women Are from Venus". Not much else happened, as I was distracted by some personal stuff.

### Master's documents

I finished the most difficult and essential part: getting recommendation letters from my professors. This involved a lot of back-and-forth communication. I also got a reference letter from my first job. Although I left that job in December 2022, I hadn't asked for one before. Finally, my motivation letter still needs some refinement, which I plan to finish next week. I'm also planning to reach out to an expert to review everything before I start applying.

### Reading

I managed to finish reading "Men Are from Mars, Women Are from Venus". I don't have much to add to what I mentioned last week. Then I started reading "Indignity: A Life Reimagined" by Lea Ypi, although I only read the first couple of pages before stopping. I've been doing some heavy reading recently, and I would like to switch to something simpler. I still haven't figured out what.

I also didn't have time to start "Hands-On Large Language Models". That's something I will kick off next week.

### PoC

Nothing much happened. Although I had plenty of time, my mind was occupied with some personal stuff that I will have to sort out as soon as possible.

### Next week

I have a few goals for next week:

- [ ] Sort out my personal issues so I can have a clear mind again.
- [ ] Reach out to an expert to review my documents for the master's applications.
- [ ] Choose a book to read next.
- [ ] Read chapter 1 of "Hands-On Large Language Models" by Jay Alammar & Maarten Grootendorst.

## Conclusion

Although not much happened, I am happy that I managed to handle a lot of correspondence this week. I still have much to do, and I haven't made much progress in several areas. I need to clear my head to be more productive again.
