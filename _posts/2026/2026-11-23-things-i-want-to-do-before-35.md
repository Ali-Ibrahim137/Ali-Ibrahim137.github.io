## Motivation

Today is my 31st birthday, so I wanted to write down eight things I would like to experience or accomplish before turning 35.

<!--more-->

## Background

Around two years ago, I wrote a similar list, but I didn't share it with anyone else. Today I am looking at the list and I see that many things have changed. I've already dropped some points; for others, I figured out that I can't do them anymore or don't want to. Now I want to add new things. It's really interesting to see how priorities change in a short time.

### 1. Find a partner and start a family

I've reached an age where I want to start looking for stability in my life. This is a thing that I won't reach without marriage and starting a family. This is something I've been trying to ignore for a long time, mostly because of other priorities and failed relationships. However, this is something that I will give high priority to in the next couple of months.

### 2. Start a business

This is another goal that I want to achieve. I am fully aware of the difficulties, and I don't want to phrase it as "start a successful business". I want to start something even if it turns out to be a complete failure. Between now and 35, I am not looking for something that would make me retire early. I need something to learn from before starting a bigger business later in my 40s.

I already have a SaaS idea that I am working on. If everything goes as planned, this might be the first thing I can mark as done before spring next year.

### 3. Write a book

That's something nice to do, but I don't feel confident enough to finish it. Writing is something I've wanted to do for a long time. I've had a couple of ideas, but never had the time to work on any of them. I remember that two years ago I started something, wrote about 15 pages, but then lost interest.

### 4. Visit +25 countries

I've already been to 12 countries:

- My home country, Syria.
- Three times to Lebanon, although it was only twice to the airport and once to the German embassy. I would still count it, as I've had the opportunity to talk with locals and walk in the streets. Lebanon is a place where I would love to spend more time in the future.
- Egypt, I've been there twice for the regional ICPC contest, the first time visiting Sharm El-Sheikh and then both Luxor and Cairo.
- UAE, I've lived there for two months.
- Germany, I've been living here since June 2023.
- Italy, my first solo journey, I've been to Abruzzo, Rome, and Sicily. It's a place that I would love to visit and live in.
- Vatican City.
- Poland, a one-day layover in Gdansk.
- Czech Republic, I've been on a day trip with my friends to Prague.
- Sweden, I've only been to Stockholm.
- The Netherlands, been there a couple of times, visiting many towns and cities, although I still haven't had the chance to spend a full day in Amsterdam.
- Iceland, an amazing country and lovely people.

I still want to travel more and extend this list to +25, with countries outside Europe if possible.

### 5. Spend +60 consecutive days in 4 different countries

This might also be one of the easiest ones. I lived in Syria until I was 27, lived ~60 days in Dubai, and I've been living in Germany since June 23, 2023. So I am only missing one other country. I was planning to spend some time in Italy this year, but I ended up with only 17 days. Probably that's something that I will achieve next year.

### 6. Speak +B1 level in 4 different languages

I already speak Arabic as a native language, English at C1 level, and a little bit of German and Russian, but barely at A1 level. This might be something challenging, but also very interesting to achieve.

### 7. Spend a night camping in a remote place

Because why not? It would be super cool and amazing :D away from distractions, phones, irritating people, alone or with only loved ones.

### 8. Do something that would make me feel proud of myself

I don't have a proper definition of this, so I just want to leave it open. It might be as simple as helping a stranger, or as complicated as running a successful business. I think when the moment comes I will know that this is it.

## Conclusion

I don't want to treat these goals as a checklist that determines whether my life is successful or meaningful. As I mentioned earlier, I wrote a similar list two years ago, and I've already discarded many of the things I wanted back then. My priorities will probably change again over the next four years, and that's perfectly fine. For now, these are simply eight things I'd love to experience or accomplish before turning 35.
